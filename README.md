An end-to-end **energy economics and data science** pipeline analyzing wholesale electricity price dynamics, volatility clustering, and fundamental market drivers—including load demand, temperature extremes, natural gas prices, and renewable energy penetration.

---

## 🎯 Business & Economic Motivation
Electricity markets exhibit unique characteristics: non-storability, inelastic short-run demand, and sharp price spikes. This project bridges the gap between **structural economic interpretability** and **out-of-sample predictive accuracy** by pairing classical econometrics (OLS) with gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost).

### Core Capabilities
* **Domain-Specific Feature Engineering:** Constructs Heating/Cooling Degree Days (**HDD/CDD**), rolling price volatility, calendar effects, and autoregressive lag features.
* **Market Mechanics & EDA:** Quantifies volatility clusters, cross-commodity correlations (gas-to-power linkage), and the **Merit Order Effect** of renewable generation.
* **Leakage-Free Modeling:** Implements a strict **time-based train/test split** to evaluate forecasting performance realistically without look-ahead bias.
* **Actionable Interpretation:** Translates statistical coefficients and feature importances into clear energy market insights.

### 2. Economic Takeaways
* **Gas-to-Power Pass-Through:** Natural gas prices remain the dominant marginal price setter, with a positive and statistically significant elasticity.
* **Merit Order Effect:** Higher renewable generation systematically suppresses wholesale prices and shifts the supply curve to the right.
* **Weather Sensitivity:** Non-linear temperature spikes (captured via HDD/CDD) drive extreme demand regimes and price volatility.
